Keyboard

  • Space or ArrowUp: hold to thrust up, release to stop
  • Enter or Space: start
  • P or Esc: pause or resume
  • M: mute or unmute
  • R: instant restart

Mouse or touch

  • Press and hold the play area to thrust
  • Release anywhere to stop thrust
  • Use the on-screen Start, Restart and Mute buttons
