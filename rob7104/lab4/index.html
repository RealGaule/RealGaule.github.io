\[ x(t) \;=\; \htmlClass{term t-passive}{e^{At}x_0} \;+\; \int_0^t e^{A(t-\tau)}\,\htmlClass{term t-forced t-controlled}{g(\tau)}\,d\tau \]

One formula for the whole lab: what the system does alone, plus what the forcing adds.

Passive

\(g = 0\). Nobody touches it. The system does whatever \(e^{At}\) says: it swings in its modes and, with friction, settles.

Forced

\(g(t)\) is given. The world pushes. Every push starts its own free motion, and the response is their sum.

Controlled

\(g = Bu\), and we pick \(u\). Which states can the motor reach, and what is the cheapest way to get there?

Part 1 · Passive

Let it swing

A drone hovers with a two-link slung load. Nothing drives it: \(\dot x = Ax\).

Part 2 · Forced

Shake it

Now the support moves on its own schedule: \(\dot x = Ax + g(t)\).

Part 3 · Controlled

Steer it

We choose the forcing: \(\dot x = Ax + Bu\), with one motor.
